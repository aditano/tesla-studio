- Downloaded original FBX from Sketchfab (72,672 triangles, 4 meshes). Inner source is labeled “Tesla Cybertruck mid-poly”; mesh nodes are named `desirefxme_*`.

Tesla Studio changes: axis normalization to metres with +Y up and -Z forward; length scaled to 5.683 m; width squeezed to 2.21 m because the export is wider than the production body; stainless MeshPhysical material; lossless meshopt compression; static presentation wrapper. Glass, doors and tonneau are not separated in the source.

This is not the higher-detail Sketcher production mesh.
